## A Funny Story About Lukasz

Once upon a time, Lukasz decided to become a master chef. He confidently entered his kitchen and announced to his cat, Mr. Whiskers, "Today I shall create the world's most amazing spaghetti!"

Lukasz carefully read the recipe: "Boil water, add pasta, cook for 10-12 minutes." Simple enough, he thought. However, Lukasz being Lukasz, he decided to "optimize" the process.

"Why wait 12 minutes when I can cook it in 2 minutes at maximum heat?" he reasoned. He cranked the stove to full blast and threw in the pasta.

Within moments, his kitchen looked like Mount Vesuvius had erupted. Pasta water bubbled over everywhere, the smoke alarm started screaming, and Mr. Whiskers had evacuated to the neighbor's house.

But the best part? When Lukasz finally served his "al dente" creation, the spaghetti had somehow achieved the consistency of concrete. His dinner guests needed a hammer and chisel to eat it.

From that day forward, Lukasz stuck to ordering pizza. And Mr. Whiskers? He learned to hide whenever he heard the words "I'll just cook something quick."

The end.

*P.S. Lukasz now has the phone number for every pizza place within a 20-mile radius memorized.*
