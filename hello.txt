## A Funny Story About a Bug

Once upon a time, there was a programmer who spent three hours debugging their code, only to discover that the bug was caused by a missing semicolon. But here's the twist: they were coding in Python!

It turns out they had been staring at their JavaScript project in another tab the whole time. The Python code was working perfectly fine. They had been debugging the wrong language entirely!

The programmer learned two valuable lessons that day:
1. Always check which tab you're working in
2. Coffee is essential before debugging sessions

And they all coded happily ever after... until the next bug appeared. 🐛
